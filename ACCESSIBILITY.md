# D'Antay's Portfolio
A small overview over changes made to the site to provide more accessibility.

## Accessibility Overview
I've identified through [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) it is important that touch targets
are properly spaced to allowed your users to easily click buttons.

## Issue
The issue was found at navigation list. The link buttons were too close together which made clicking on those
targets a slight issue. I fixed it by adding more margin between the buttons, which made that bar a bit more 
accessible.
